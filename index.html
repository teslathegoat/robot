About

Security Researcher & Lifelong Learner

I'm fascinated by complex systems, unconventional thinking, and problems that challenge established assumptions. What started as an interest in finding vulnerabilities gradually evolved into a deeper curiosity about how systems are designed, where trust breaks down, and why things fail.

I believe in understanding over memorization, curiosity over credentials, and questioning over accepting things at face value.

I'm not just interested in finding what's broken. I'm interested in understanding why it breaks, how it happens, and what we can learn from it.

My goal is to keep exploring, researching, and contributing to a deeper understanding of technology and security.
